Viva Questions and Answers for Job Portal HTML

Question: What is the purpose of <!DOCTYPE html>?
Answer: It declares the document type and version of HTML, helping browsers render the page correctly.

Question: What does the <html lang="en"> tag do?
Answer: It defines the root of the HTML document and sets the language to English.

Question: What is the function of the <head> section?
Answer: The <head> contains meta-information about the document, such as character encoding, viewport settings, and the title.

Question: What does <meta charset="utf-8" /> specify?
Answer: It sets the character encoding to UTF-8, supporting most characters and symbols.

Question: Why is <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0" /> important?
Answer: It ensures the page is responsive and scales correctly on different devices.

Question: What does the <title> tag do?
Answer: It sets the title of the web page, shown in the browser tab.

Question: What is the role of the <body> tag?
Answer: It contains all the visible content of the web page.

Question: What is the purpose of the <header> tag?
Answer: It defines the header section, typically containing the site title and navigation.

Question: What does <h1> represent?
Answer: <h1> is the main heading of the page, indicating the most important title.

Question: What is the use of the <nav> tag?
Answer: <nav> defines a navigation section with links to different parts of the site.

Question: Why are <ul> and <li> used inside <nav>?
Answer: <ul> creates an unordered list, and <li> defines each list item, used here for navigation links.

Question: What is the <section role="search"> for?
Answer: It marks a section for searching jobs, with a heading, description, and search form.

Question: What does the <form> tag do?
Answer: It creates a form for user input, allowing job searches.

Question: What is the purpose of <label> and <input> in forms?
Answer: <label> describes the input field, and <input> collects user data.

Question: What does the <select> tag do?
Answer: It creates a dropdown menu for selecting experience level.

Question: What is the <main> tag used for?
Answer: <main> contains the main content of the page, such as job listings and filters.

Question: What is the function of <aside>?
Answer: <aside> contains tangential content, like filters, related to the main content.

Question: What does <details> and <summary> do?
Answer: <details> creates expandable sections, and <summary> provides the clickable heading.

Question: What is the difference between <input type="checkbox"> and <input type="radio">?
Answer: Checkbox allows multiple selections; radio allows only one selection in a group.

Question: What is the <section> inside <main> for?
Answer: It displays featured job listings.

Question: What does the <article> tag represent?
Answer: <article> marks self-contained content, like individual job postings.

Question: What is the use of <header> inside <article>?
Answer: It provides a heading and company info for each job post.

Question: What does the <img> tag do?
Answer: It displays company logos for each job listing.

Question: What is the purpose of <h3> and <p> inside job articles?
Answer: <h3> shows the job title, and <p> provides company and job details.

Question: Why is <ul> used in job articles?
Answer: To list job attributes like experience, salary, location, and tags.

Question: What does the <footer> inside <article> do?
Answer: It provides a link to view more details about the job.

Question: What is the role of the <footer> at the end of the page?
Answer: It contains copyright information and links to privacy policy and terms of service.

Question: What is the significance of semantic HTML tags like <header>, <nav>, <main>, <aside>, <section>, <article>, and <footer>?
Answer: They improve accessibility, SEO, and code readability by clearly defining the structure and meaning of content.
